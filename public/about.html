About Us

Digital Support Systems builds software that helps security integrators design, survey, and accurately estimate video surveillance and access control systems.

Digital Support Systems

Our mission

Designing a surveillance or access control system involves countless moving parts — sites and buildings, camera locations and coverage, hardware models, cabling, labor, and the proposals and budgets that tie them together. Too often that work lives in scattered spreadsheets and disconnected notes, making estimates slow to produce and easy to get wrong.

Our mission is to bring that entire process into one place. We give integrators the tools to capture what they see in the field, turn it into an accurate scope of work, and deliver professional proposals with confidence — so they can spend less time on paperwork and more time protecting what matters.

What we do

Our flagship platform, the VMS Estimator, is an end-to-end tool for planning and pricing video surveillance and access control projects. It connects the field survey, system design, and the final estimate into a single, structured workflow:

  • Site & building management Organize customers, sites, and buildings, and attach supporting documents and floor plans to keep every project's records in one place.
  • Camera & device planning Lay out camera locations, mounting details, coverage purpose, and device models across each building.
  • Field surveys Capture site surveys — including photos and voice-driven notes — directly from the field as you walk the site.
  • Access control estimating Account for access methods, hardware, and the line items that make up a complete access control scope.
  • Cost & fee modeling Build project costs and fee summaries that roll site detail up into transparent, defensible numbers.
  • Proposals & invoices Generate polished, client-ready proposals and invoices straight from the project data you've already entered.
